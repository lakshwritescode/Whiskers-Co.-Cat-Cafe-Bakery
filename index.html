06 / GOOD TO KNOW

Before you
come by.

Do I need to book?

Walk-ins are welcome when space allows, but a reservation guarantees your lounge time and a table in the cafe.

Can children visit?

Absolutely. Guests under 12 need an adult with them, and everyone gets a quick gentle-cat orientation at the door.

Can I adopt a cat?

Yes. Our rescue partners handle every adoption conversation so each match is thoughtful and supported.

Do you have quiet hours?

Tuesday mornings are our softest hours, designed for reading, working, and visitors who prefer a calmer room.
